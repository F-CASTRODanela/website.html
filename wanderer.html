The Wanderer

I think of myself as a wanderer of sort a person who is a wanderer of the body, mind, and will. am

omeone who embraces the 'unfamilliar'finds value in the pathways of a journey, and relishes the opportunities that change and the possibility of 'the different' presents.

For me, wandering is not the state of being lost it is the state of being open. Open to new experiences, new people, new ideas, and new parts of myself. I ask questions and legions of wonders entangle me, always striving to unveil the mysteries just beyond the horizon a new place, a different view, or a new self that lies in wait.

I find comfort in movement, and am, even the road or a trail is not well defined. i have learned to live with a lack of focus. i have listened to internal intuition, and learned that every leg of a journey, every idiosyncrasy, and every mobility, even when blind, has a value. Meaning is not derived from the planning of a course or a leg.

I appreciate gatherings but cherish moments more a conversation that changed me, a place that stayed with me, a realization that changed how I perceive the world. I value solitude, but I also treasure the connection that happens when you meet someone in a strange place and feel strangely understood.

Wandering is different. I may not always have a single point of focus. but I always have intention. I with no plan, am at ease with starting over, am at ease with shopping what is no longer right. I am a process of becoming and I let the world add what I honesty, openness, and wonder
