The Wanderer

I think of myself as a wanderer of sort a person who is a wanderer of the body, mind, and will.

I am someone who embraces the unfamiliar, finds value in the pathways of a journey, and relishes the opportunities that change and the possibility of the different presents.

For me, wandering is not the state of being lost—it is the state of being open. Open to new experiences, new people, new ideas, and new parts of myself.

I find comfort in movement, even when the road or a trail is not well defined. Every part of a journey has value.

I appreciate gatherings but cherish moments more—a conversation, a place, or a realization that changed me.

Wandering is a process of becoming. I grow, explore, and let the world shape me with honesty and wonder.
